### Procedural, low-poly, open.

- Terrain generated with FastNoiseLite; Y-snapping keeps NPCs and props planted on the ground.
- Dynamic day-night cycle on a 180-second loop; sun, shadows, and ambient light all shift.
- Springarm camera, 3D navigation, forward-plus renderer — all stock Godot 4.4.

The vibe is Kenney and Quaternius low-poly, not photoreal. Small polycounts, strong silhouettes, readable at a glance.

<!-- content:quests -->
### Interact, talk, advance.

- Hover a prop → press E → dialogue tree opens.
- Three objective kinds: `TALK_TO_NPC`, `COLLECT`, `GO_TO_LOCATION`.
- Quest states flow INACTIVE → ACTIVE → COMPLETED → TURNED_IN; dialogue reacts to state.

Classic RPG loop, minimalist UI. No quest markers on a mini-map — you read NPC dialogue and pay attention.

<!-- content:sprints -->
### AI sprints ship the game.

- The Tado Eternal loop spawns sprints; each sprint parses headlessly, boots the scene, self-scores.
- Every merged commit is gated on a `godot --headless --quit-after 3` build.
- Metrics live in `.tado/eternal/metrics.jsonl` — build_clean + scene_boots + milestone_progress + polish_delta.

Noble is the experiment: can an AI agent author and ship a real 3D game? So far: slowly, yes.

## Seven-milestone roadmap

Noble currently sits at **M4 (quest system)** of seven milestones. The plan:

1. **M1 — Foundation.** Scene, player controller, terrain, camera. Done.
2. **M2 — Interactable world.** NPCs, landmarks, hover-prompt system. Done.
3. **M3 — Dialogue.** Dialogue tree UI, state machine, NPC lines keyed to quest state. Done.
4. **M4 — Quests.** Objective kinds, quest log, quest bootstrap. In progress.
5. **M5 — Inventory + collection.** Pickupable items, inventory UI, gear slots.
6. **M6 — Day-night systems.** Sleep, world events, time-gated quests.
7. **M7 — Polish + ship.** Audio, tuning, packaging, itch.io release.

The roadmap is in `CLAUDE.md` and drives every sprint's brief.

## Assets and credits

Noble uses CC0-only assets — nothing that restricts redistribution.

- Kenney low-poly packs (nature, village, characters).
- Quaternius stylized props and creatures.
- Custom shaders and scripts authored inside the sprint loop.

Attribution lives in [`SOURCES.md`](https://github.com/cumulus/noble/blob/main/SOURCES.md). When Noble ships, the full game is downloadable with every asset's license intact.
